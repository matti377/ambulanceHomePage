The short version

No account. No personal-data collection. Your preferences remain on your device.

01

Data collection

Charlie does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or account details. The app does not require you to create an account.

02

Device permissions

Charlie may ask for permission to send notifications. Permission is optional and can be changed at any time in your device settings.

Notifications Used to let you know about new calls.

03

Data storage

App settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. Charlie does not upload these settings to its own external servers. Local data remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

04

Platform services

When notifications are enabled, delivery may rely on services provided by your device or operating-system provider. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies.

05

Security

We take reasonable measures to help Charlie operate securely. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Charlie evolves. Any changes will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised.
